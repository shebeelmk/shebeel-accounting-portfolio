The working approach

Structured, transparent and built around your business.

Good accounting is more than entering numbers. It is a repeatable process that keeps records clear and helps you spot what needs attention.

STEP 01

Understand & organise

Review your current records, software, transaction volume and reporting needs before agreeing on a scope.

STEP 02

Record & reconcile

Maintain records, match bank activity and organise balances and supporting documents consistently.

STEP 03

Report & review

Share agreed reports, highlight outstanding items and prepare schedules for VAT or audit review as applicable.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before we begin.

If your requirements are different from a standard package, we can discuss a suitable scope first.

Do you work with businesses outside the UAE?

Remote support may be possible depending on the accounting software, records, local compliance requirements and agreed scope.

Are the package prices fixed for every business?

No. They are starting prices for routine work. Transaction complexity, record quality, inventory requirements and reporting needs can affect the final quote.

Do you provide VAT and Corporate Tax services?

I can support accounting records, reconciliations, VAT workings and filing-related preparation within the agreed scope. Any regulated service or representation must be handled only where legally authorised and appropriately qualified.

Can you help clean up previous accounting records?

Yes, after reviewing the period, number of transactions and current records. Historical cleanup is normally quoted separately from a monthly package.

What do you need to start?

Usually, your transaction records, bank statements, sales and purchase documents, existing accounting balances and a short discussion about your reporting needs.
